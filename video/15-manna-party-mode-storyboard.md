# Manna Party Mode — Social Video Storyboard

- Format: vertical 9:16 TikTok, Reels, and YouTube Short
- Runtime: 24 seconds
- Brand colors: deep navy `#0F1223`, gold `#D4A843`, and white
- Audience moment: four adult friends playing after dinner

## Narration

> Game night just changed. One person starts a Manna Party. Up to twelve players join on the same Wi-Fi. Everyone gets the same Bible question and locks in their own answer. Then Manna reveals who got it right and keeps the score. Turn dinner, youth group, or any hangout into a Bible challenge. Download Manna on iPhone and Android.

## Scenes

1. **0–4 seconds — Hook and host**
   Four friends raise their phones around an after-dinner table. The host starts the game.
   On-screen copy: `ONE ROOM. ONE QUESTION.` then `CREATE A PARTY`.

2. **4–8 seconds — Join locally**
   Four gold phone outlines connect around a Wi-Fi symbol.
   On-screen copy: `UP TO 12 PLAYERS • SAME WI-FI`.

3. **8–12 seconds — Shared challenge**
   Everyone receives the same question and selects an answer on their own phone.
   On-screen copy: `SAME QUESTION • YOUR OWN ANSWER`.

4. **12–16 seconds — Reveal and scoring**
   Friends celebrate and groan playfully as the correct-player result appears.
   On-screen copy: `SEE WHO GOT IT RIGHT`.

5. **16–21 seconds — Social payoff**
   The group laughs and prepares for the next question.
   On-screen copy: `MANNA PARTY MODE`.

6. **21–24 seconds — Call to action**
   Navy-and-gold Manna end card with Apple and Android availability.
   On-screen copy: `DOWNLOAD MANNA`.

The live app captures used to validate the feature and advertising claims are stored in `video/assets/party-mode/`. They show the Party Mode menu, four-player lobby, shared question, individual answers, and correct-player reveal.
