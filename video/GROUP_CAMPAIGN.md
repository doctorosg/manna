# Manna Group Campaign — Five Social Video Concepts

All concepts are designed for TikTok and YouTube Shorts in vertical 9:16 format, with captions placed above the platform UI safe zone. Brand colors are deep navy `#0F1223`, gold `#D4A843`, and white.

## 1. Dinner's Over. Game On.

- Audience: multigenerational family
- Setting: dining table immediately after dinner
- Length: 20 seconds
- Hook: `DINNER'S OVER. GAME ON.`
- Voiceover: “Dinner’s over—but nobody’s leaving. We opened Manna, picked a category, and suddenly everyone had something to prove. Kids versus parents. Layperson versus Pastor. Who really knows the Bible best? Turn any table into game night. Download Manna and play together.”

## Creative safeguards

- Maintain the same identifiable people, clothing, setting, and lighting across every scene in each video.
- Show people taking turns reading from phones; do not imply synchronized multiplayer.
- Do not display invented readable app interfaces.
- Avoid fake reviews, ratings, download counts, timers, coins, or testimonials.
- Phrase the Noah fact carefully: Genesis distinguishes clean animals and birds from animals that were not clean; do not say every animal entered by sevens.
