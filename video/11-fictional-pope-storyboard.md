# Manna Short 11 — “Read It to Know It”

## Creative direction

- Format: vertical 9:16 TikTok and YouTube Short
- Target duration: 15 seconds
- Tone: warm, clever, respectful comedy
- Character: fictional older actor in generic white ceremonial religious clothing and white skullcap; no resemblance to any real Pope, no Vatican insignia, no endorsement
- Setting: comfortable after-dinner game night with four adults taking turns playing Manna on phones
- Brand: deep navy `#0F1223`, gold `#D4A843`, white
- Captions: large burned-in captions positioned above the lower platform UI safe zone

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook (0–2.5s)

- Visual: The fictional pope-style character sits confidently among friends, holding a phone and smiling as everyone waits for his answer.
- On-screen hook: `HE SHOULD KNOW THIS…`
- Audio: A playful suspense sting begins.

### Scene 2 — Question (2.5–6s)

- Visual: One player asks, “What fruit did Eve eat?” The ceremonial character answers confidently, “An apple.”
- On-screen choices: `APPLE • FIG • FRUIT • GRAPE`
- Camera: Tight reaction shots; do not show an invented readable app interface.

### Scene 3 — Reveal (6–9.5s)

- Visual: A friendly red X appears. Another player smiles and says, “The Bible only says fruit.” The ceremonial character reacts with genuine, good-natured surprise.
- On-screen reveal: `THE BIBLE ONLY SAYS “FRUIT”`
- Tone: Everyone laughs with him, never at him.

### Scene 4 — Brand payoff (9.5–12s)

- Visual: Quick group reaction montage and a natural glimpse of people continuing the Manna game.
- Voiceover: “Even the most confident answer can be wrong.”
- On-screen: `HOW WELL DO YOU KNOW THE BIBLE?`

### Scene 5 — CTA (12–15s)

- Visual: Clean navy-and-gold Manna end card.
- Voiceover and exact tagline: “You have to read the Bible to know the Bible.”
- On-screen: `DOWNLOAD MANNA` and `iPhone + Android`

## Production safeguards

- State in the prompt that the character is fictional and must not resemble any living or historical Pope.
- Do not use Vatican crests, official seals, recognizable papal locations, or endorsement language.
- Keep the joke centered on overconfidence and Bible knowledge, not on the character’s religion or dignity.
- Keep the same identifiable cast, clothing, room, and lighting in every shot.
- Do not imply synchronized multiplayer; show players taking turns.
- No fake reviews, ratings, download counts, timers, coins, subscriptions, or invented readable Manna screens.
