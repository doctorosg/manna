# Manna Short 12 — Fictional Pope-Style Grand Hall

## Non-negotiable visual

A completely fictional older actor is visibly dressed in a recognizable pope-style ceremonial outfit: full white cassock, short white shoulder cape, white skullcap, gold pectoral cross, and polished white shoes. He must not resemble any living or historical Pope. He sits alone in an ornate, oversized, high-backed ceremonial chair in a magnificent fictional marble audience chamber with tall columns, classical marble statues, gold details, and soft daylight. He holds a smartphone clearly in one hand.

## Format

- Vertical 9:16
- Target duration: 16–18 seconds
- Cinematic, photorealistic, gently comedic
- Same actor, costume, chair, phone, statues, room, and lighting in every shot
- No Vatican insignia, official seals, real papal locations, real Pope likeness, or endorsement

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Establish the character (0–3s)

- Wide cinematic reveal of the marble hall, statues, and grand chair.
- The fictional pope-style character sits in the chair holding his phone.
- On-screen: `EVEN HE GETS ONE WRONG…`

### Scene 2 — Question appears (3–7s)

- Medium close-up over the phone, keeping the costume and marble room visible.
- A clean floating question card appears beside him: `What fruit did Eve eat?`
- Choices: `Apple • Fig • Fruit • Grape`
- He reads aloud: “What fruit did Eve eat?”

### Scene 3 — Wrong selection (7–10s)

- He confidently taps `Apple`.
- The `Apple` choice becomes visibly selected.
- A distinct wrong-answer buzzer sounds and a red X appears.

### Scene 4 — Reaction (10–12.5s)

- Close-up of his surprised face, still seated in the grand chair with phone in hand.
- He says clearly: “No way!”
- On-screen answer reveal: `The Bible only says “fruit.”`

### Scene 5 — Manna payoff (12.5–18s)

- Transition to a clean navy-and-gold Manna end card.
- Exact voiceover: “You have to read the Bible to know the Bible. Test your Bible knowledge with Manna—40,000 questions.”
- On-screen:
  - `YOU HAVE TO READ THE BIBLE TO KNOW THE BIBLE.`
  - `TEST YOUR BIBLE KNOWLEDGE WITH MANNA`
  - `40,000 QUESTIONS`
  - `iPhone + Android`
  - `Fictional dramatization. No endorsement implied.`

## Audio

- Grand but light orchestral opening
- Spoken question must be audible
- Phone-tap sound
- Clear comedic wrong-answer buzzer
- Spoken “No way!” reaction
- Music ducks under all dialogue and the final voiceover
