# Manna Short 13 — Pastor-Level Surprise

## Verified Manna question

- Question ID: `a41021c25aaf`
- Category: Paul & His Letters
- Difficulty: Pastor
- Question: `What does the Greek word “Parakletos” mean?`
- Choices:
  - A. Save us, we pray
  - B. Helper, Advocate, Comforter
  - C. The Word
  - D. God with us
- Correct answer: `B. Helper, Advocate, Comforter`
- Fictional character’s wrong selection: `D. God with us`

## Format and continuity

- Vertical 9:16 TikTok and YouTube Short
- Target duration: 22–25 seconds
- Same fictional older actor, full white pope-style ceremonial outfit, white skullcap, gold cross, grand high-backed chair, phone, marble statues, columns, and lighting throughout
- No resemblance to a real Pope; no Vatican insignia, real location, or endorsement

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Pastor level (0–3s)

- Wide reveal of the fictional marble chamber and statues.
- The fictional pope-style character is seated in the grand chair, holding his phone and opening Manna.
- On-screen: `PASTOR LEVEL`

### Scene 2 — He reads the question and choices (3–12s)

- Medium shot with his phone clearly visible.
- The question card floats beside him while he reads aloud:
  - “What does the Greek word Parakletos mean?”
  - “A: Save us, we pray.”
  - “B: Helper, Advocate, Comforter.”
  - “C: The Word.”
  - “D: God with us.”
- All choices remain readable on screen.

### Scene 3 — Confident wrong answer (12–15s)

- He smiles knowingly, as though certain of the answer.
- He taps `D. God with us` on his phone.
- A loud wrong-answer buzzer sounds immediately.
- A red X appears and the correct choice is highlighted: `B. Helper, Advocate, Comforter`.

### Scene 4 — Extreme surprise (15–18s)

- Tight close-up while he remains seated and holding the phone.
- He looks extremely shocked and exclaims loudly: `Wrong?! No way!`
- His eyebrows rise; he looks back and forth between the phone and the room.

### Scene 5 — Voice from above (18–23s)

- The character remains on screen, visibly perplexed, looking upward and around the marble chamber.
- A distinctly different, deep, powerful, reverberant off-screen voice from above says exactly: `You have to read the Bible to know the Bible.`
- No visible second speaker.
- On-screen tagline matches the voice exactly.

### Scene 6 — CTA (23–25s)

- Navy-and-gold Manna end card.
- On-screen: `TEST YOUR BIBLE KNOWLEDGE WITH MANNA`, `40,000 QUESTIONS`, `iPhone + Android`.
- Small disclaimer: `Fictional dramatization. No endorsement implied.`

## Audio requirements

- The fictional character audibly reads the entire question and all four choices.
- A subtle tap sound accompanies the wrong selection.
- A loud, unmistakable game-show-style wrong-answer buzzer follows immediately.
- His `Wrong?! No way!` must sound extremely surprised, not casual.
- The voice from above must be a separate performance: much deeper, louder, slower, and reverberant, with the music ducked beneath it.
