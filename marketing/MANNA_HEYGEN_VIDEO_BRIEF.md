# Manna Launch Videos — HeyGen Production Brief

## Campaign idea

**The Manna Challenge: How well do you really know the Bible?**

The creative opens with a playable Bible question, delivers a surprising answer, and then reveals Manna as the way to keep testing and learning. The tone is warm, intelligent, respectful, and inviting—never preachy, combative, or game-show cheesy.

## Exact product claims

- 50,000 permanently numbered questions, including 10,000 children's questions
- 21 categories
- Three difficulty levels: Layperson, Deacon, Pastor
- Five questions per session
- Untimed play
- Performance by category and difficulty
- Personalized study suggestions after sufficient play
- 22 supported languages
- Available for Apple and Android
- Free, ad-supported experience with an optional one-time Remove Ads purchase
- No subscription

Do not introduce different prices, timers, rankings, multiplayer, wagering, tokens, subscriptions, testimonials, download counts, ratings, or endorsements.

## Brand and production direction

- Brand colors: deep navy `#0F1223`, gold `#D4A843`, white
- Pronunciation: **Manna = MAN-uh**
- Presenter: warm, credible adult, approximately 30–50; conversational delivery; direct eye contact; modest neutral clothing; no clergy costume
- Presenter treatment: TikTok uses a natural creator-style presenter or green-screen overlay. YouTube uses presenter only at the opening and close, with real app footage carrying the middle.
- Music: subtle uplifting instrumental, modern acoustic/percussion texture, no recognizable hymn or copyrighted song
- Captions: burned in, high contrast, sentence case, maximum two lines
- Product footage: use only real Manna screen recordings. Never generate or reconstruct the interface with AI.
- Do not show advertising or purchase screens in the launch montage.
- Do not use a fake user testimonial or have an AI avatar say, “I use Manna.”

## Required real app captures

Record clean clips without notification banners or touch indicators:

1. Home screen with MANNA and the question count
2. Category grid scrolling through several categories
3. Layperson / Deacon / Pastor difficulty selection
4. The Eve fruit question with all four answers visible
5. Selection of “The Bible just says ‘fruit’ — it doesn’t say what kind”
6. Correct-answer reveal and explanation/reference
7. End-of-session results
8. My Performance: Categories
9. My Performance: Improve
10. Language picker scrolling through several languages

# Video 1 — TikTok / Reels / YouTube Short

## Concept: “The Fruit Test”

**Master length:** 26 seconds  
**Format:** 1080 × 1920, 9:16, 30 fps  
**Style:** creator-led challenge, quick but readable  
**Primary goal:** comments, shares, installs

### Scene 1 — Hook

**Time:** 0:00–0:02.5  
**Visual:** Presenter fills the frame. Fast push-in. The Manna question screen is faintly visible behind the presenter.  
**Voiceover/presenter:** “Quick—what fruit did Eve eat in the Garden of Eden?”  
**On-screen text:** `DON'T ANSWER TOO FAST 👀`  
**Direction:** Begin speaking on the first frame. No logo animation before the hook.

### Scene 2 — Play the question

**Time:** 0:02.5–0:07.5  
**Visual:** Full-screen real Manna question UI. Show the exact four choices. Add a small three-beat countdown graphic, but do not cover any answer.  
**Voiceover:** “Apple, fig, just ‘fruit,’ or grape? Three… two… one.”  
**On-screen text:** `What’s your answer?`  
**Sound:** Three soft countdown ticks.

### Scene 3 — Surprising payoff

**Time:** 0:07.5–0:11  
**Visual:** Tap and reveal the correct Manna answer: “The Bible just says ‘fruit’ — it doesn’t say what kind.” Brief gold highlight.  
**Voiceover:** “It never says apple. Scripture only says fruit.”  
**On-screen text:** `The answer is C: “fruit”`  
**Direction:** Let the reveal breathe for at least one full second.

### Scene 4 — Product reveal

**Time:** 0:11–0:15  
**Visual:** Presenter appears as a green-screen overlay in the lower-left while the real Manna category and difficulty screens move behind them.  
**Voiceover:** “That kind of surprise is exactly what Manna is built for.”  
**On-screen text:** `Meet MANNA`  
**Direction:** Keep the presenter clear of the app’s important controls.

### Scene 5 — Proof montage

**Time:** 0:15–0:21.5  
**Visual:** Three rapid real-app clips: category grid, difficulty levels, performance/study suggestions. Use gold match cuts.  
**Voiceover:** “Test yourself with 50,000 questions, including new games for children, plus 21 adult categories and three adult levels.”
**On-screen text, one card at a time:**

- `50,000 QUESTIONS`
- `21 CATEGORIES`
- `LAYPERSON • DEACON • PASTOR`

### Scene 6 — CTA

**Time:** 0:21.5–0:26  
**Visual:** Presenter points toward the Manna app icon and Apple/Android badges. End on the Manna navy-and-gold lockup.  
**Voiceover:** “How well do you really know the Bible? Download Manna and comment your first score.”  
**On-screen text:** `DOWNLOAD MANNA`  
**Secondary text:** `iPhone + Android • No subscription`  
**Direction:** Hold the final CTA for at least 1.5 seconds.

# HeyGen generation instructions

1. Build every numbered scene as a separate editable HeyGen scene.
2. Upload and place the real app capture specified for that scene before generating narration.
3. Use an AI presenter only in the scenes that explicitly request one.
4. Use exact supplied narration. Do not let Video Agent rewrite product claims.
5. Add captions manually from the approved narration and proofread every Bible term.
6. Keep TikTok text within the center safe area, away from the right-side interaction buttons and bottom caption region.
7. Keep app screens large enough to read; crop rather than placing an entire tiny phone in the middle of the frame.
8. Use clean cuts and restrained gold transitions. Avoid floating Bibles, glowing crosses, excessive heavenly imagery, or generic church stock footage.
9. Normalize voice levels and keep music approximately 18–22 dB below narration.
10. Render draft versions first. Check text accuracy, timing, pronunciation, source footage, and CTA before using credits on final-quality exports.

## Final review checklist

- Manna is pronounced MAN-uh
- “Layperson,” “Deacon,” and “Pastor” are spelled correctly
- Exact figures are 50,000 questions, 21 adult categories, three children's age groups, and 22 languages
- No timer is shown or implied
- No unsupported testimonial, rating, download count, or endorsement appears
- All app footage is from the real current build
- Captions do not cover questions, answer choices, store badges, or the CTA
- TikTok is 9:16; YouTube is 16:9
- Both versions end with the correct live store destination or pre-registration destination
