# Retired question banks

`children_questions_legacy_kjv_template_2026-09-01.json` is the complete
10,000-question children's bank retired on 2026-09-01. It is preserved exactly
as shipped before the age-appropriateness rebuild. Its question IDs and
permanent numbers remain reserved in `question_number_registry.json`; they must
never be assigned to replacement questions.

The bank was retired rather than bulk-moved into an adult level. Its simple
two-choice items are generally below Layperson difficulty, while its obscure
book/section identification items range from Deacon to Pastor. It also contains
passages that require individual editorial review before any adult
reclassification. Nothing in this archive is part of the active app bank.
